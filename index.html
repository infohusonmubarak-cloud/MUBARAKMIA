About

Hi, I'm Hossain — an aspiring Computer Science student.

I'm preparing to study Computer Science at university, building toward that goal through self-directed coding, tutoring Math and Physics, and translation work.

Alongside that, I tutor SAT, Math and Physics students and translate between Burmese, English and Rohingya for NGOs and community organizations — work that's shaped how I think about breaking down complex problems and communicating clearly, both things I want to keep building on in a Computer Science program.

I'm self-taught in web development so far. Applying to university is about building a more formal, rigorous foundation in computer science on top of the practical skills I've picked up on my own.
